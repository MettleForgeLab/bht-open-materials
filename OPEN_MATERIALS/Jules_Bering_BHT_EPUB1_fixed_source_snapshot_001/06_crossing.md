# Crossing

A distinction may outgrow the posture that first revealed it.

Crossing changes posture, not origin.

A threshold object prepares passage.
An answerable trace preserves enough relation for another meeting to recognize what arrived without pretending the earlier meeting remained present.

Carry:

what arrived;
where it came from;
its source, scope, and qualification;
what work has already occurred;
what changed in passage;
what was omitted or did not travel;
what remains uncertain;
what status it carries;
what consequence is now permitted;
who retains authority;
where return remains possible.

Possibility must not arrive as proof.
Structure must not arrive as necessity.
Local result must not arrive as universal rule.
Prepared form must not arrive as lived fact.

The receiving gravity may change the work.
It must be able to tell what it received.
