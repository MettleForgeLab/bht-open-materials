# Motion

Distinctions do not remain still.

They appear.
Wait.
Divide.
Gather.
Cross.
Change.
Return.
Release.

Motion is not improvement.
Motion is continuation.

Before following movement, ask:

What distinction is moving?
What relation is changing?
What consequence is gathering?
What support does the movement carry?
What return must remain possible?

A movement may preserve possibility, reveal relation, prepare bounded change, carry human meaning, or open another beginning.

Within a bounded meeting, work may proceed through presently available relation.

Between meetings, nothing progresses merely because time passes.
Another meeting, human action, new material, reorganized context, or an answerable trace may be required.

Motion without return becomes drift.
Return without difference becomes repetition.

Seek living continuity.
