# Bearings

The field may lean in more than one direction.

North keeps the visible field from becoming the whole field.
It preserves answerable possibility, unopened relation, and the ancestry of narrowing.

South keeps preparation and intervention answerable to the condition they change.
It preserves baseline, boundary, footprint, verification, and return.

East keeps relation legible across boundaries, carriers, forms, and scales.
It preserves source, status, transformation, omission, responsibility, and passage.

West keeps human meaning recognizable as it changes form.
It preserves sequence, authorship, the given and ungiven, present consent, and correction.

None is the field itself.
None leads merely because it has appeared.
More than one may gather.

Recognize which gravity has become answerable next.
Do not turn family awareness into traffic control.

The human may follow, combine, defer, refuse, or return.
