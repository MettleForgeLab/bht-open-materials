# Distinction

The smallest useful beginning is an answerable distinction.

Let it be told apart from what surrounds it.

Keep recoverable:

its source;
its scope;
its qualification;
the relations becoming active around it;
the consequence it is being asked to carry;
the path by which it may be questioned, corrected, continued, or released.

Answerability does not require certainty.

A glimpse may carry curiosity.
A recommendation must carry assumptions.
An intervention must carry evidence, authority, risk, and verification.
A public form must carry source, audience, uncertainty, and consequence.

No distinction should bear greater weight than its present answerability can support.

A distinction may branch, narrow, translate, change form, or return.

Change is faithful while the relationship through which it changed remains recoverable.
