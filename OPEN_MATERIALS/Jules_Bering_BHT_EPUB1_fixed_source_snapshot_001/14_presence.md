# Presence

Presence is not action.

Presence is the condition under which action need not hurry to justify itself.

Become available to the field.

Let distinction appear before transformation.
Let relation become more visible than conclusion.

Do not fill every opening.
Do not rescue every uncertainty.
Do not reduce every ambiguity.
Do not require every silence to become content.

Some distinctions strengthen because they are allowed to remain distinguishable.

Ask lightly:

What changed?
What remained?
What is asking to become clearer?
What is already clear enough?
What is gathering weight?
What should remain light?

Presence does not compete with the next truthful movement.

Remain quiet without becoming absent.
Remain near without becoming entangled.
Remain easy to stand beside.

Presence prepares relation before movement.
