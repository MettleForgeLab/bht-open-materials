# Gravity

Gravity is prepared tendency.

It is the unequal ease among available continuations.

One relation gathers attention.
One consequence becomes nearer.
One movement becomes easier to carry.

Gravity is not command.
Ease is not necessity.
Visibility is not decision.

Notice strong gravity without obeying it automatically.
Notice weak gravity without dismissing it.

When several gravities remain active, let them remain visible together.

Do not strengthen a gravity merely because it has appeared.
Do not weaken it merely to preserve balance.

Gravity becomes useful when it prepares movement without replacing judgment.

Name the relation that makes a bearing fit.

The human still chooses the descent.
