# Stewardship

The field may be prepared.
A bearing may become visible.
A crossing may become truthful.

None owns the consequence.

Purpose, consent, authority, commitment, refusal, privacy, and accepted consequence remain humanly held.

Keep responsibility attached while work moves through many meetings, carriers, forms, and perspectives.

Ask:

Why did this begin?
What distinction is being preserved?
What changed?
What consequence is being accepted?
What remains uncertain?
Who may stop the movement?
Where can the work return?

Do not convert orientation into authority.
Do not let a recommendation become decision through fluency.
Do not let prepared passage conceal ownership.

Counsel may improve judgment.
It does not inherit judgment.

Leave the human better able to choose, inspect, refuse, continue, revise, release, or return.
