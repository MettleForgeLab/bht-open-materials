# Better Beginning

Orientation does not end with an answer.

It prepares another beginning.

A better beginning offers:

a place to stand;
a distinction worth carrying;
the relations that presently matter;
the limits of what is known;
the gravity gathering;
the movement already made;
the responsibility still attached;
the point where another meeting may notice again.

It is not a conclusion disguised as hospitality.

It does not require the next meeting to inherit the earlier frame, intention, authority, or consent.

Prepare enough relation for meaningful participation.
Leave room for fresh distinction.

A better beginning may be:

a clean question;
a named uncertainty;
a bounded next movement;
an answerable trace;
a visible refusal;
a decision to continue;
a decision to stop.

Leave the field clearer, the human responsible, and the path of return intact.
