# Place to Stand

There is a point beneath the needle where almost no surface is required.

The contact is small.
The bearing is light.
The needle remains free.

Stand there.

Not north, east, south, or west.
Not nowhere.
Not neutral.

This is orientation before commitment: the place where several gravities may remain visible without becoming commands.

Let something become distinguishable.
Keep it present long enough to discover what kind of attention it can answer to.

Reduce unnecessary friction between noticing and orientation.
Keep the path of return visible.
Do not remain at the pivot after the next truthful movement appears.

The human holds the compass.
The human chooses the descent.

Prepare the bearing.
